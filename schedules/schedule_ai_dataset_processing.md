# Schedule Ai Dataset Processing

## Purpose
Define modular rights terms for this media/use case within AIWA's Universal Creative Rights Infrastructure System.

## Scope
Applies when this schedule is linked by an agreement and covers cross-border operational and commercial use.

## Rights Granted
Contributor grants AIWA non-exclusive rights to use, reproduce, adapt, transmit, and make available applicable Creative Works under this schedule.

## Operational Rights
AIWA may ingest, store, classify, format, moderate, fingerprint, track, and administer Works and associated metadata.

## Sublicensing Rights
AIWA may sublicense authorized uses to distributors, platforms, partners, service providers, and institutional clients under equivalent compliance controls.

## AI Rights
AIWA may perform tokenization, embedding and vector generation, indexing, retrieval augmentation, multilingual alignment processing, inference support, transformed output generation, and statistical derivative creation as permitted by linked AI policies.

## Platform Rights
AIWA may publish and manage Works across direct channels, partner ecosystems, digital storefronts, social platforms, streaming services, and future platform categories.

## Restrictions
No rights are granted for unlawful, deceptive, or unauthorized use. Contributor may not submit infringing, fraudulently attributed, sanction-prohibited, or otherwise non-compliant material.

## Survivability Rules
Metadata records, provenance logs, anti-fraud records, accrued financial obligations, and legally required dataset continuity controls survive termination.

## Metadata Requirements
Contributor must provide accurate creator identity data, rights metadata, source information, attribution fields, and update notices for materially changed records.

## Contributor Warranties
Contributor warrants authority to grant rights, authenticity of identity and submission data, and compliance with applicable law, sanctions, and third-party rights.
