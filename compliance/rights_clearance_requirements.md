# Rights Clearance Requirements

## Purpose
Set institutional compliance controls supporting rights defensibility and operational enforceability.

## Scope
Applies to onboarding, rights intake, partner operations, cross-border transactions, and enforcement support.

## Control Requirements
- Verification workflows aligned to anti-fraud and provenance controls.
- Documentation standards suitable for audits, disputes, and platform enforcement.
- Escalation procedures for sanctions, identity, rights, and data-integrity flags.

## Survivability
Compliance records and legal hold material survive termination as required by law, active dispute, or enforcement necessity.
