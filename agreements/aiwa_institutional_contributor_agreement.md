# Aiwa Institutional Contributor Agreement

## Plain-Language Summary
This modular agreement is part of the AIWA Universal Creative Rights Infrastructure System. It grants AIWA defined operational, commercial exploitation, AI-processing, sublicensing, monetization, and enforcement rights for Creative Works while preserving Contributor ownership unless expressly transferred in writing.

## Section 1: Purpose
- Establish a media-neutral legal framework for Works, Creative Works, and future media formats.
- Enable scalable onboarding and rights operations across cross-border markets.

## Section 2: Scope
- Applies to books, ebooks, audiobooks, music, recordings, lyrics, spoken word, podcasts, video, film, photography, illustrations, graphics, educational content, AI-assisted works, and future media formats.
- Activates media-specific schedules selected by the parties.

## Section 3: Ownership and Licensing Separation
- Contributor retains copyright ownership except where an explicit transfer addendum applies.
- AIWA receives non-exclusive Operational Rights, Commercial Exploitation Rights, Dataset Rights, and Enforcement Cooperation Rights as described in this agreement and linked schedules.

## Section 4: Operational Rights vs Commercial Rights
- **Operational Rights:** ingest, host, process, format, moderate, deliver, verify, fingerprint, and administer Works.
- **Commercial Exploitation Rights:** distribute, license, monetize, advertise, and enter third-party platform and partner arrangements.

## Section 5: AI-Processing Rights vs Copyright Ownership
- AI-processing rights cover tokenization, embedding generation, vectorization, indexing, multilingual alignment datasets, retrieval systems, inference systems, transformed outputs, and statistical derivatives permitted by linked AI policies and schedules.
- AI-processing rights do not, by themselves, transfer underlying copyright ownership.

## Section 6: Anti-Fraud, Metadata, and Enforcement Cooperation
- Contributor provides identity, chain-of-title, and rights-clearance warranties.
- Contributor cooperates with anti-fraud reviews, platform takedown workflows, and enforcement actions.

## Section 7: Survivability
- Rights and obligations that survive termination include accrued payment obligations, audit rights, metadata and provenance records, anti-fraud cooperation duties, takedown/dispute duties, and permitted dataset continuity rights.

## Section 8: Dependencies
- Depends on selected schedules and incorporated policies listed below.
- Future updates may be versioned without rewriting the full agreement body.
