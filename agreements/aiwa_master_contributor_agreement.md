# AIWA Master Contributor Agreement

Version: v1.0-draft
Status: Universal Creative Rights & AI Infrastructure Agreement System

## PART I — Plain Language Summary

This summary is for readability only. The operative terms are in the legal clauses below.

### What the Contributor keeps
- The Contributor keeps copyright ownership in the Works unless a separate written transfer addendum expressly says otherwise.
- The Contributor keeps all rights that are not expressly licensed to AIWA.
- The Contributor may continue to exploit the Works elsewhere on a non-exclusive basis, subject to any schedule-specific exclusivity elected later in writing.

### What AIWA receives
- AIWA receives a non-exclusive, worldwide, sublicensable right to onboard, host, process, distribute, commercialize, market, protect, and administer the Works through AIWA services and authorized third parties.
- AIWA receives a separate set of operational rights, distribution rights, commercial rights, AI-processing rights, metadata rights, and enforcement cooperation rights.
- AIWA may authorize distributors, digital service providers, social media services, publishers, marketplaces, sync licensees, educational platforms, and enterprise customers to exercise the licensed rights needed to deliver or monetize the Works.

### AI rights summary
- AIWA may ingest the Works and Contributor Materials into AI systems for indexing, fingerprinting, moderation, search, recommendation, translation, rights administration, analytics, accessibility, dataset generation, training, retraining, and inference operations as allowed by this Agreement and applicable schedules.
- AIWA may create embeddings, vectors, statistical representations, multilingual alignment data, and transformed datasets derived from the Works.
- If AIWA has already built or updated a model, dataset, embedding index, or other transformed AI asset during the term, the Contributor cannot require AIWA to reverse engineer, delete, or untrain that asset except where mandatory law requires otherwise.

### Payment summary
- AIWA will account for revenue actually received from exploitation of the Works after deducting agreed commissions, third-party fees, taxes, payment processing costs, refunds, reversals, reserves, fraud holds, and other deductions authorized by this Agreement or the applicable schedule.
- Some uses may generate no direct payment.
- AIWA may withhold or reserve funds for disputes, fraud review, suspected manipulation, chargebacks, DSP clawbacks, or legal compliance.

### Termination summary
- Either party may terminate future exploitation under this Agreement as stated below, but AIWA may continue to fulfill pre-termination commitments, wind down active licenses, collect receivables, maintain records, and preserve surviving AI, metadata, audit, and enforcement assets.
- Removal from future services may take time because third-party platforms, caches, and sublicenses operate on operational timelines.

### AI survivability summary
- Identifiable copies of Works can be removed from future active services where operationally practicable and legally permitted.
- Transformed datasets, embeddings, model weights, fraud signals, provenance records, and statistical derivatives may survive termination when already created, required for system integrity, or reasonably necessary for compliance, auditing, defense, retraining continuity, or active commercial obligations.

### Anti-fraud warning
- AIWA may suspend Works, pause onboarding, delay releases, withhold payments, place reserves, request evidence, fingerprint content, investigate ownership, and cooperate with platforms when AIWA detects fake streams, metadata fraud, AI impersonation, synthetic voice misuse, manipulated ownership claims, unauthorized samples, identity fraud, or other abuse.

### Contributor responsibilities
- The Contributor must submit accurate metadata, maintain rights clearances, avoid infringing or deceptive content, cooperate with investigations, and keep payment and tax information accurate.
- The Contributor must not use AIWA to launder manipulated traffic, fraudulent engagement, impersonation assets, or unlicensed training material.

## PART II — Definitions

For purposes of this Agreement:

### 1. Works
"Works" means all content, files, media, expressions, and deliverables that the Contributor submits, uploads, authorizes, links, or otherwise makes available to AIWA now or later, including all supported and future digital media formats.

### 2. Literary Works
"Literary Works" means books, ebooks, manuscripts, articles, essays, scripts, lyrics, educational text, captions, and other text-based or text-dominant works.

### 3. Sound Recordings
"Sound Recordings" means master recordings, stems, mixes, performances fixed in audio, podcasts, audiobooks, spoken-word recordings, voice assets, and other audio-only works.

### 4. Audiovisual Works
"Audiovisual Works" means film, video, animation, livestream captures, trailers, visual podcasts, interactive video, and other synchronized audio-visual works.

### 5. Visual Works
"Visual Works" means photographs, illustrations, graphics, cover art, designs, still images, thumbnails, motion graphics frames, and other image-based works.

### 6. Metadata
"Metadata" means all descriptive, technical, administrative, commercial, rights-management, provenance, identity, usage, fingerprint, and transactional information associated with Works or Contributor Materials.

### 7. AI Datasets
"AI Datasets" means organized corpora, training sets, evaluation sets, moderation sets, retrieval sets, alignment sets, localization sets, rights-management sets, and other machine-readable collections derived from or containing Works, Contributor Materials, Metadata, or transformed representations of them.

### 8. Embeddings
"Embeddings" means vectors, token representations, latent representations, hashes, fingerprints, and other machine-readable mathematical encodings generated from Works, Contributor Materials, or Metadata.

### 9. Derivative Outputs
"Derivative Outputs" means translations, excerpts, previews, accessibility formats, transcodes, thumbnails, captions, summaries, recommendations, generated tags, synthetic metadata, analytics outputs, model outputs, and other derivative or transformed materials created through authorized exploitation or AI processing of the Works.

### 10. Platform Services
"Platform Services" means AIWA's contributor onboarding, storage, rights administration, metadata governance, licensing, monetization, analytics, settlement, moderation, trust and safety, anti-fraud, provenance, enforcement, and related services.

### 11. UGC Services
"UGC Services" means user-generated-content platforms, creator ecosystems, short-form media services, creator monetization services, fan-content environments, and similar platforms that enable uploads, remixing, sharing, or creator monetization.

### 12. Distribution Services
"Distribution Services" means digital or physical distribution, streaming, download, delivery, publishing, syndication, retail, broadcast, educational distribution, marketplace listing, and enterprise delivery services.

### 13. AI Systems
"AI Systems" means machine learning, generative AI, retrieval, ranking, recommendation, moderation, search, translation, synthesis, inference, rights-detection, identity-verification, fraud-detection, and analytics systems, whether developed by AIWA or by authorized third parties.

### 14. Commercial Exploitation
"Commercial Exploitation" means any monetized or revenue-oriented use of the Works or Derivative Outputs, including sales, streams, subscriptions, advertising, sync, sponsorship, licensing, sublicensing, white-label delivery, enterprise access, AI commercialization, marketplace transactions, and data products.

### 15. Contributor Materials
"Contributor Materials" means Works, Metadata, names, likenesses, biographical material, brand assets, identifiers, artwork, stills, press materials, tax forms, account information, rights documents, and other materials supplied or authorized by the Contributor.

## PART III — Grant of Rights

### 16. General structure of the grant
**Scope.** Subject to this Agreement and the selected schedules, the Contributor grants AIWA the separate rights stated in Sections 17 through 21 on a non-exclusive, worldwide, transferable as part of a business transfer, and sublicensable basis.

**Operational purpose.** The grant is designed to separate copyright ownership from AIWA's operational, commercial, AI, metadata, and enforcement functions.

**Contributor obligations.** The Contributor must select accurate schedules, territories, and commercial options and must not enable uses the Contributor is not authorized to grant.

**Limitations.** Rights are limited to the categories expressly granted here and any narrower limits in applicable schedules or policies.

**Survivability.** Survives only as stated in Part VIII and the specific subsections below.

### 17. Operational Rights
**Scope.** AIWA may receive, ingest, upload, store, host, cache, transcode, encode, normalize, edit for technical quality, format, package, index, archive, preserve, fingerprint, hash, watermark, segment, synchronize, translate for accessibility or localization, and otherwise technically process the Works and Contributor Materials.

**Operational purpose.** These rights exist to provide Platform Services, maintain service integrity, enable discovery, preserve chain of custody, administer rights, support audits, and meet platform-delivery requirements.

**Contributor obligations.** The Contributor must provide deliverables in required formats, accurate technical metadata, and any necessary cue sheets, split data, credits, or rights documentation.

**AIWA rights.** AIWA may use service providers, cloud systems, content delivery networks, preservation vendors, and identification vendors to perform these functions.

**Limitations.** Operational edits may not materially distort the substantive content of a Work except where required for law, policy, technical compliance, safety, or accessibility.

**Survivability.** Archival copies, fingerprints, preservation files, audit logs, and integrity records may survive termination for recordkeeping, dispute resolution, fraud prevention, and legal compliance.

### 18. Distribution Rights
**Scope.** AIWA may distribute, publish, deliver, communicate to the public, make available, stream, download, broadcast, syndicate, list, print on demand, transmit, and otherwise exploit the Works through Distribution Services and UGC Services.

**Operational purpose.** These rights enable placement of the Works on DSPs, marketplaces, publisher channels, audiobook services, podcast services, bookstores, social platforms, educational services, and future digital media channels.

**Contributor obligations.** The Contributor must maintain clearance for each distribution format, territory, language version, sample, featured performance, and third-party element.

**AIWA rights.** AIWA may appoint aggregators, publishers, distributors, resellers, collecting intermediaries, social media partners, and platform operators to exercise these rights.

**Limitations.** Distribution is subject to platform rules, territory limits, format-specific schedules, and availability constraints. AIWA is not required to distribute every Work or keep any Work continuously available.

**Survivability.** AIWA may continue distribution for the reasonable wind-down period needed to honor pre-termination commitments, collect accrued revenue, remove inventory, and process platform takedowns.

### 19. Commercial Rights
**Scope.** AIWA may license, sublicense, sell, resell, bundle, monetize, advertise, promote, offer subscriptions, negotiate sync uses, authorize branded uses, administer UGC monetization, enable marketplace offers, and otherwise engage in Commercial Exploitation of the Works and authorized Derivative Outputs.

**Operational purpose.** These rights allow AIWA to operate as a cross-media licensing and monetization platform rather than a traditional label or publisher.

**Contributor obligations.** The Contributor must disclose restrictions, moral rights issues, collecting society conflicts, union obligations, embargoes, and third-party approvals that may affect monetization.

**AIWA rights.** AIWA may set pricing, packaging, promotional positioning, release timing, bundling, offer structures, and commercial terms with third parties, provided that settlements remain subject to this Agreement and any schedule-specific economics.

**Limitations.** AIWA may not transfer ownership of the Works without a separate written transfer addendum. Exclusive commitments require a separate written addendum.

**Survivability.** Existing licenses, receivables, audit rights, reserve rights, and payment adjustments survive as long as reasonably required to conclude or enforce transactions entered during the term.

### 20. AI Rights
**Scope.** AIWA may use the Works, Contributor Materials, and Metadata for ingestion, parsing, tokenization, segmentation, feature extraction, fingerprinting, vectorization, embedding generation, multilingual alignment, named-entity extraction, classification, moderation, rights verification, search, retrieval, ranking, translation, summarization, recommendation, personalization, analytics, model evaluation, dataset generation, training, fine-tuning, retraining, reinforcement, safety tuning, and inference operations.

**Operational purpose.** These rights support AIWA's AI-native rights infrastructure, including rights administration, discovery, anti-fraud, recommendation, data products, marketplace intelligence, and lawful AI commercialization.

**Contributor obligations.** The Contributor must disclose any legal or contractual restriction that limits AI processing, biometric processing, or training uses of the Works.

**AIWA rights.** AIWA may create, combine, label, clean, annotate, transform, store, license, and commercialize AI Datasets, Embeddings, and Derivative Outputs derived from authorized inputs; may combine the Works with other lawfully controlled materials; and may use AI Systems operated by AIWA or authorized third parties.

**Limitations.** Unless a schedule states otherwise, this Section does not authorize AIWA to falsely attribute generated outputs to the Contributor or to use the Contributor's name or likeness as an endorsement outside the Promotional Rights granted in Section 21.

**Identifiable works vs transformed datasets.** Identifiable copies of Works remain subject to this Agreement's content, removal, and rights-management rules. Transformed datasets, Embeddings, model weights, fraud signals, statistical relationships, and similar non-human-readable or non-substitutable AI assets are treated as operational AI infrastructure rather than substitute copies of the Works.

**Survivability.** AI Datasets, Embeddings, training artifacts, safety layers, fraud models, statistical derivatives, evaluation corpora, retraining assets, and model updates created during the term may survive termination to preserve dataset continuity, model integrity, auditability, security, legal compliance, and active downstream commitments. The Contributor may terminate new discretionary future ingestion after termination, but may not revoke, unwind, or require reconstruction of already-built models or transformed AI assets except where mandatory law expressly requires otherwise.

### 21. Promotional Rights
**Scope.** AIWA may use the Works, titles, excerpts, previews, thumbnails, artwork, approved names, logos, likenesses, credits, and Metadata to market the Works, AIWA services, and authorized commercial opportunities.

**Operational purpose.** These rights support discovery, audience growth, platform promotion, sales support, and partner enablement.

**Contributor obligations.** The Contributor must provide accurate branding materials and promptly notify AIWA of any legal restriction on publicity materials.

**AIWA rights.** AIWA may create trailers, snippets, samplers, captions, promo copy, case studies, storefront pages, pitch decks, and partner-facing marketing materials.

**Limitations.** AIWA will not knowingly use promotional materials in a deceptive manner or as a false endorsement of unrelated products.

**Survivability.** AIWA may keep archival promotional records and continue limited use necessary to wind down active campaigns and preserve transaction histories.

## PART IV — Ownership, Reservation of Rights, and Moral Rights Position

### 22. Ownership
The Contributor retains ownership of the Works except to the extent an express written transfer addendum states otherwise.

### 23. Reservation of rights
All rights not expressly granted remain with the Contributor.

### 24. No traditional label relationship
This Agreement creates a rights infrastructure relationship. It does not by itself make AIWA the employer, manager, collecting society, or statutory representative of the Contributor.

### 25. Moral rights and credit position
To the extent permitted by applicable law, the Contributor consents to technical, formatting, accessibility, localization, promotional, moderation, and AI-processing changes authorized by this Agreement, provided such changes are not intentionally derogatory. AIWA may rely on supplied credits and may correct manifest metadata errors.

## PART V — Metadata, Provenance, and Governance

### 26. Metadata license and governance authority
**Scope.** The Contributor grants AIWA the right to collect, verify, normalize, enrich, merge, display, correct, store, and share Metadata and provenance records related to the Works and Contributor Materials.

**Operational purpose.** To support rights administration, discovery, settlements, conflict checks, fraud review, partner delivery, provenance tracking, and chain-of-title management.

**Contributor obligations.** The Contributor must provide complete and current Metadata, including authorship, ownership splits, contributors, sample data, publishing data, collection conflicts, and usage restrictions.

**AIWA rights.** AIWA may append AI-generated tags, fraud signals, IDs, fingerprints, content labels, and distribution-specific fields and may keep historical versions for audit integrity.

**Limitations.** AIWA may not knowingly publish confidential payment or identity documents except as required for operations, compliance, or enforcement.

**Survivability.** Metadata histories, provenance logs, audit trails, and conflict-resolution records survive termination for integrity and compliance purposes.

## PART VI — Anti-Fraud, Trust and Safety, and Enforcement Cooperation

### 27. Verification and anti-fraud controls
AIWA may require identity verification, age checks where applicable, ownership evidence, source files, session data, usage logs, provenance materials, and fraud-prevention information before or after onboarding.

### 28. Prohibited conduct
The Contributor must not submit or support fake streams, manipulated engagement, metadata fraud, AI impersonation, synthetic voice fraud, deceptive cloning, unauthorized samples, manipulated ownership claims, copyright fraud, identity fraud, sanctions evasion, or other abusive conduct.

### 29. Enforcement authority
**Scope.** AIWA may review, flag, suspend, reject, delist, geoblock, mute, demonetize, reserve against, or remove Works; delay settlements; issue notices; submit disputes; send takedowns; use fingerprinting systems; and cooperate with DSPs, marketplaces, payment providers, and legal authorities.

**Operational purpose.** To protect platform integrity, respond to claims, prevent abuse, and enforce rights granted or administered under this Agreement.

**Contributor obligations.** The Contributor must respond promptly to requests for evidence, cure inaccurate metadata, cooperate in disputes, and not interfere with platform investigations.

**Limitations.** AIWA acts as a private rights infrastructure operator, not as a governmental authority. AIWA will apply escalation measures on a proportionate, documented, and operationally scalable basis.

**Survivability.** Investigation records, fraud models, reserves, clawback rights, and cooperation obligations survive termination until the relevant issue is closed.

### 30. Reserves, holds, and clawbacks
AIWA may establish reserves and withhold or recoup amounts for claims, fraud review, suspected policy breaches, chargebacks, refunds, DSP reversals, tax obligations, sanctions review, or accounting errors.

## PART VII — Revenue, Accounting, and Payment

### 31. Revenue model
Revenue may arise from distribution income, publishing income, sync, UGC monetization, subscriptions, advertising, enterprise licensing, sublicensing, AI commercialization, marketplace licensing, educational licensing, and future monetization models.

### 32. Settlement basis
Unless a schedule provides otherwise, net revenue means amounts actually received by AIWA from exploitation of the Works minus commissions, third-party fees, taxes, payment processing costs, currency conversion costs, refunds, chargebacks, reserves, fraud losses, uncollectible receivables, and legally required deductions.

### 33. Payment timing
AIWA may pay on periodic settlement cycles after receipt of funds and completion of fraud, tax, compliance, and threshold checks.

### 34. Non-payment situations
The Contributor acknowledges that certain promotional uses, beta services, internal AI operations, moderation operations, safety training, and non-monetized availability may generate no separate payment unless a schedule states otherwise.

### 35. Audit and corrections
AIWA may correct good-faith accounting errors, offset overpayments against future amounts, and maintain records reasonably sufficient to support settlements.

## PART VIII — Term, Suspension, Termination, and Survival

### 36. Term
This Agreement starts when accepted and continues until terminated under this Part VIII.

### 37. Suspension
AIWA may suspend any Work, feature, payout, territory, or service access immediately for legal, policy, fraud, security, quality-control, sanctions, identity, ownership, or operational reasons.

### 38. Contributor termination of future services
The Contributor may terminate future exploitation on written notice, subject to reasonable operational wind-down, existing third-party commitments, accrued obligations, and survival rights.

### 39. AIWA termination
AIWA may terminate or decline future exploitation at any time for convenience, business, compliance, risk, legal, or policy reasons.

### 40. Effect of termination
Termination stops new discretionary future exploitation unless continued use is necessary to wind down activities, satisfy sublicenses, collect receivables, complete reporting cycles, preserve compliance records, or exercise surviving rights.

### 41. Survival
The following survive termination for as long as reasonably necessary for their purpose: payment rights and adjustments; audit rights; reserves; fraud and enforcement cooperation; Metadata and provenance records; privacy and compliance duties; dispute handling; rights needed to honor pre-termination commitments; and the AI Rights survivability provisions in Section 20.

## PART IX — Contributor Representations, Warranties, and Responsibility Allocation

### 42. Contributor representations
The Contributor represents, warrants, and covenants that:
- the Contributor has the right and authority to enter this Agreement and grant the rights granted here;
- the Works and Contributor Materials, as delivered, do not knowingly infringe third-party rights or violate applicable law;
- all Metadata and payment information supplied to AIWA is materially accurate and current;
- required consents, clearances, licenses, split agreements, and permissions have been obtained;
- the Contributor will not upload malware, deceptive files, or intentionally corrupted assets; and
- the Contributor will comply with incorporated policies and selected schedules.

### 43. AI-assisted and third-party materials disclosure
The Contributor must disclose material AI generation, synthetic voice use, training-source restrictions, third-party samples, public-domain dependencies with attribution requirements, and any other circumstance that could affect exploitation, authenticity, or legal risk.

### 44. Indemnity framework
To the extent permitted by applicable law, the Contributor will indemnify AIWA and its authorized partners against third-party claims arising from breach of the Contributor's representations, unauthorized submissions, or fraud by the Contributor. AIWA will control defense and settlement of claims relating to platform operations, while reasonably informing the Contributor where the Contributor's conduct is at issue.

## PART X — Privacy, Compliance, and Jurisdiction Framework

### 45. Privacy and compliance
AIWA may process personal data and compliance information as described in incorporated policies and applicable law, including KYC, AML, sanctions, tax, and recordkeeping obligations.

### 46. Cross-border operations
The parties acknowledge that AIWA operates across African markets, ECOWAS-connected environments, and global partner ecosystems, and that data, rights administration, and dispute handling may involve multiple jurisdictions.

### 47. Dispute structure
Unless a schedule or mandatory law states otherwise, disputes should first be escalated through AIWA's documented review procedures, then commercial negotiation, and then arbitration or court proceedings designated in the applicable jurisdiction schedule or contributor onboarding terms.

### 48. Flexible jurisdiction strategy
This Agreement is intended to support jurisdiction-specific schedules rather than assume a single U.S.-centric legal model.
