# Creator Identity Policy

## Purpose
Set standalone compliance and operating rules incorporated by reference into AIWA agreements and schedules.

## Scope
Applies to Contributors, guardians (where relevant), partners, and platform operators handling Creative Works and related metadata.

## Core Policy Rules
- Media-neutral application across supported Works and future formats.
- Cross-border compliance support with ECOWAS relevance and international platform operations.
- Recordkeeping requirements for rights verification, fraud detection, and enforcement response.

## Survivability
Audit records, provenance data, active dispute records, and legal compliance obligations survive termination to the extent required by law or active claims.
