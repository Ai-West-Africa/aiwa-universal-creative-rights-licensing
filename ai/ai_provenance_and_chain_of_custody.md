# Ai Provenance And Chain Of Custody

## Purpose
Define explicit, modular AI rights and controls for legally defensible processing of Creative Works and related data assets.

## Scope
Applies to model training corpora, machine learning models, inference systems, retrieval systems, multilingual alignment datasets, and related processing pipelines.

## Technical Rights Definitions
- **Tokenization:** segmenting source content into processable units.
- **Embeddings/Vectors:** numerical representations used for indexing, retrieval, clustering, and similarity operations.
- **Transformed Outputs:** outputs materially transformed from source expressions by authorized systems.
- **Statistical Derivatives:** aggregate learned parameters and probabilistic artifacts that do not constitute direct ownership transfer of source copyrights.

## Rights and Limitations
- AIWA receives Dataset Rights necessary for lawful ingestion, preprocessing, model training support, evaluation, retraining, and inference operations within agreement scope.
- Rights are conditioned on provenance verification, consent controls, and policy compliance.

## Survivability
Permitted continuity of non-reversible operational derivatives, audit logs, and legally required records survives termination subject to agreement and law.
