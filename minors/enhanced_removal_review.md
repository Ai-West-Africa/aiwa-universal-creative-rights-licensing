# Enhanced Removal Review

## Purpose
Define age-sensitive protections and enforceable safeguards for minor-related participation in AIWA workflows.

## Scope
Applies where a Contributor is a minor, or where content features a minor requiring heightened protections.

## Core Safeguards
- Verified guardian consent is required before activation of commercial or dataset-related rights.
- Unauthorized exploitation is prohibited.
- Enhanced removal and review pathways apply to minor-related requests.
- Age-sensitive handling rules govern publication, monetization, and AI processing permissions.

## Survivability
Consent records, withdrawal records, and legal safeguard logs survive termination for legal defense and compliance tracking.
